Terms of service

This page identifies the commercial and operational terms required before Falcon POS is offered to customers.

Draft for product development. These terms are not legally approved. Pricing, service levels, payment processing responsibilities, governing law and the operating company identity must be finalized before launch.

Account and authorized use

The final terms must define owner authority, tenant responsibilities, acceptable use, staff access and the security obligations of business administrators.

Subscription and service

The final terms must define plans, billing, renewals, cancellation, feature availability, support scope, maintenance and service-level commitments.

Business and store data

Businesses retain responsibility for the accuracy and legality of operational data entered into the service. Ownership, export and deletion procedures must be defined.

Payments and liability

If payment processing is introduced, the final agreement must identify the licensed payment provider, settlement responsibilities, disputes, refunds and applicable limitations of liability.
