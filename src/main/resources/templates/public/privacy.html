Legal foundation

Privacy notice

This page outlines the privacy topics the production Falcon POS notice must cover.

Draft for product development. This is not approved legal advice and must be completed with the operating company name, address, support email, hosting regions, subprocessors and applicable jurisdictions before launch.

Information categories

Falcon POS may process account identity, business and store configuration, staff access, sales transactions, payments metadata, inventory movements, device and security logs, and support communications when the corresponding services are implemented.

Purposes

Information should only be used to provide the service, secure accounts, support retail business operations, meet legal obligations and improve authorized product functionality.

Retention and security

The final policy must define retention periods, deletion procedures, encryption controls, access restrictions, incident response and backup practices.

User rights and contact

The final policy must describe access, correction, export and deletion rights and provide the verified privacy contact for the operating company.
