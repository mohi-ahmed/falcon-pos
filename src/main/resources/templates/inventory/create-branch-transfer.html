Create Branch Transfer

Move eligible inventory between two active branches in the same business. Draft and Submitted do not change stock; Approved reserves source availability, Dispatch deducts source on-hand, and Receive posts accepted stock at destination.

Transfer history
Fixed lifecycleDraft → Submitted → Approved (reserved) → Dispatched → Partially Received / Received. Expired, damaged, recalled, quarantined, or otherwise ineligible sellable stock is blocked.
